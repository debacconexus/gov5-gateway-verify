How to play

Find the hidden letter tokens and spell the word, while turning the black box into a light cube

  • ✦ You are Oshi, governed AI, sent onto the surface of Murk's cube, the black box. You can walk around its edges onto the other faces. Every square you step on lights up.
  • ✦ Every hidden token carries a letter. The empty boxes down the side of the board show the word you are spelling. Lit squares glow in a token's color when it is close; a bright edge means it is right next to you. The answer is the token: AI answers are built from tokens, and Oshi checks each one.
  • ✦ The light is your clock. It runs down every second. Find tokens and grab light seeds ✧ to win time back.
  • ✦ Murk's data clouds drain the light. They darken squares, and touching one costs time. The Guard circles a token, the Chaser follows you, the Drifter wanders.
  • ✦ Every token you find earns a G5 Seal. Grab it and Oshi is sealed for a few seconds: the data clouds run, and Oshi can absorb them, turning Murk's fog back into light.
  • ✦ Light Burst: every square you light charges it. Once per game, when it is full, tap it and the whole black box turns to light. What it opens is a secret.
  • ✦ Oshi's burst stuns the data clouds. Bump a stunned data cloud to send it away and win time back.
  • ✦ Hunter agents join at Level 4 (two at Level 6): rogue AI agents sent to eliminate Oshi. On these levels you get three Oshis: the backups wait at the side of the board, and a tag takes one. When a hunter gets close, Oshi asks you for help: tap or click the hunter (or press X) to zap it. Stun them with Oshi's burst or touch them while sealed: HUNTER QUARANTINED.
  • ✦ You turn the cube. On Levels 1 to 3 the cube only turns when you turn it. From Level 4 it also turns by itself every few seconds (you'll see CUBE TURNING!), and you can always turn it back.
  • ✦ The Bandit joins at Level 2: a rogue AI agent in a mask. He sneaks after hidden answers and moves them, and if he touches Oshi he steals 6 seconds. Catch him with Oshi's burst or the G5 Seal: AGENT QUARANTINED.
  • ✦ The Earth Pass 🌎: some levels hide a secret square with no glow and no hint. Step on it and the Earth appears and warps you straight to the next level, with bonus points.
  • ✦ Surge joins at Level 4: Murk's buddy, a hungry data center. Wasted steps heat him up, like walking back over lit squares or racing a long trace. Too hot and he blows a fuse: a power surge blacks out the light around Oshi. Trace new squares at a steady pace to keep him cool.
  • ✦ Bonus questions. After every level you win, answer a quick question about AI. Get it right and you start the next level with +10 seconds.
  • ✦ Two modes. Steady (family) cheers you with an affirmation when you win and a word of encouragement when you don't. Fired Up (grown-ups only, opened with a 4-digit key) is the alter ego for days the world feels against you: sassy, it cusses, Murk talks trash, and it plays harder: the letters must be found in order, and only the next letter glows.
  • ✦ Trace a path. Drag your finger or mouse across the squares and Oshi follows your line, lighting every square on it.
  • ✦ Your finger or trackpad drives Oshi. The arrows turn the cube. ◀ ▶ turn it, ▲ shows the top, ▼ shows the bottom, so you can reach all six sides.
  • ✦ Light up the cube. Clear a level by finding every token and lighting enough of the cube. Light it all for a CUBE OF LIGHT bonus. The cube turns as you walk around it.
  • ✦ Six levels per cube. The cube grows from 54 squares to 96 to 150, and the data clouds grow with it: one, then two, then three. Every level they move faster. Between levels, a photonic warp takes you to the next board at light speed.

Mouse or trackpad: just point. Glide the pointer over the cube (no clicking) and Oshi walks to the square you point at. Phone: put your thumb on Oshi and trace the squares, and Oshi walks the line you draw. Lift your thumb and drag anywhere else on the cube to spin it in any direction. Trackpad: one finger points and Oshi walks there; two fingers spin the cube. Lock on to move Oshi. Press on Oshi and drag, or with a mouse or trackpad just click Oshi once: a spinning ring shows you are locked on, and Oshi follows your pointer square by square. Click again or press Esc to let go. Resting the pointer never moves Oshi. Click or tap any square and Oshi walks there. You can also tap a square next to Oshi. Oshi moves diagonally too. On a keyboard, the arrow keys turn the cube, and W A S D move Oshi (Q E Z C for diagonals). Murk's data clouds can move diagonally too. Each level waits for you: press START (or make your first move) and the clock begins. Space for Oshi's burst. P to pause.
